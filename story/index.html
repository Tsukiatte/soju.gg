About this episode

What happens (spoilers)

They meet every day after that, and she seems to know him already. She always leaves before midnight, and she cries at the happiest moments. One night she leaves her diary behind, and he reads it: its dates run backwards, because time runs the other way for her. His tomorrow is her yesterday, so the day they met was her last day with him, and every first for him was a last for her. He walks away, then thinks of her side of that first train ride, her last day, when he looked at her like a stranger, and goes back. On their last night they wait on the platform where they met, and at midnight she is gone. This time, the tears are his.

Retold from the film Tomorrow I Will Date With Yesterday's You (2016), directed by Takahiro Miki. The characters, sets and sound effects are our own: modelled and rendered in Blender, and the sound effects were made in code. The narration voice is ElevenLabs' Adam. About this episode

What happens (spoilers)

Her memories come back: she is Elizabeth Hansen, a scientist whose husband Leo caught the virus that is killing Earth. She opens the pod's window and finds she is in space, on a ship of sleeping colonists, some of their pods smashed. The old woman on the phone is the real Elizabeth. She is a clone, sent with a clone of Leo to start again on a new planet. At 5% oxygen she decides she wants to live, takes the oxygen from the dead pods, and goes back to sleep. She wakes on the shore of a new world, and Leo is there.
