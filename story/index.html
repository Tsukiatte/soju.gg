About this episode

What happens (spoilers)

Her memories come back: she is Elizabeth Hansen, a scientist whose husband Leo caught the virus that is killing Earth. She opens the pod's window and finds she is in space, on a ship of sleeping colonists, some of their pods smashed. The old woman on the phone is the real Elizabeth. She is a clone, sent with a clone of Leo to start again on a new planet. At 5% oxygen she decides she wants to live, takes the oxygen from the dead pods, and goes back to sleep. She wakes on the shore of a new world, and Leo is there.

Retold from the film Oxygen (2021), directed by Alexandre Aja. The characters, sets, narration and music are our own: modelled and rendered in Blender, narrated with the open-source Kokoro voice, and the score and sound effects were made in code.
